## Platform

web

## Users
- Pharmaceutical manufacturers, regulators and drug testing laboratories who need current standards, reference substances, notices and draft monographs open for comment.
- Healthcare professionals, researchers, students and trainees looking for training programmes, internships and pharmacovigilance resources.
- Citizens and applicants looking for careers, tenders, RTI, contact details and official announcements.

## Product Purpose
The public website of the Indian Pharmacopoeia Commission (IPC), an autonomous institution of the Ministry of Health & Family Welfare, Government of India, which sets the standards of drugs in the country. It publishes the Indian Pharmacopoeia, the National Formulary of India and IP Reference Substances, runs the Pharmacovigilance and Materiovigilance Programmes of India, and publishes notices, recruitment and training. Success: visitors find official information quickly and trust it.

## Positioning
The only official book of standards for drugs in India under the Second Schedule of the Drugs and Cosmetics Act 1940. Every fact on the site comes from www.ipc.gov.in.
